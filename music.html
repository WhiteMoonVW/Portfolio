Core songs and stories

House

Electronic / Experimental • Presented at West Fork New Music Festival, NSEME LSU • 2017

“House” represents the mind and the thoughts that dwell inside it — especially intrusive thoughts that repeat and replay. Inspired by your own inability to sleep, it also reflects your early electronic music period and love of synthesizers, layered harmonies, and chopped vocal fragments.

Iridescent

Alt Pop / Songwriter • Presented at Durango Songwriters Expo • 2020

A reflective, color-soaked track centered on perception, mind, and spiritual imagery. This is the kind of song that naturally connects to a visual world, making it a strong candidate for a future Hydra preset.

Reincarnation

Pop Ballad / Reflection • Durango Songwriters Expo • 2020

Inspired in part by the 1997 film “Selena,” this piece holds both sadness and hope — reflecting on absence, legacy, and the possibility that a spirit or life-force can continue to inspire through art.

Afterlife

Piano / Vocal Process • Recent work

A strong example of your process: hearing a melody internally, figuring it out by ear, layering piano parts in Logic, and shaping the song through performance and recording. The emotional tone reflects a deeply personal period in your life.
