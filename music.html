House

Electronic / Experimental • Presented at West Fork New Music Festival, NSEME LSU • 2017

“House” represents the mind and the thoughts that dwell inside it — especially intrusive thoughts that repeat and replay. Inspired by my own inability to sleep; reflects my early electronic music period and love of synthesizers, layered harmonies, and chopped vocal fragments.
